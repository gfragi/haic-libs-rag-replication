# Cross-domain validation study

Planned. This folder will hold the material for the cross-domain study in
the accompanying paper:

- a script that recomputes Team D's metrics from its decisions artifacts
  under `haic-metrics` 0.2.1 (the version the teams ran) and 0.3.0;
- SHA-256 checksums of every team artifact used in the paper.

The team artifacts themselves are not published here; they are available
from the author on request, subject to the participants' consent terms.
